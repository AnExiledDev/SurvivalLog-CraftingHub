Name: Crafting Hub
Summary: Workbenches use every storage in your home, make intermediate materials for you, and craft a queue of different recipes. Recipes you could make after crafting their intermediates are marked.

Description (BBCode):
A workbench normally only uses its linked storages and makes one recipe at a time. Crafting Hub lets every workbench use every storage in your home, makes the planks and parts in between for you, and crafts a whole queue of recipes in order.

[size=5]Main features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Every home storage counts[/b]: workbenches count and take materials from every storage in your home.[/*]
[*][b]Craftable through intermediates[/b]: recipes you could make after crafting their intermediates get a dashed amber edge and a hammer badge. Select one to see what gets made first.[/*]
[*][b]Craft with intermediates[/b]: one button makes the intermediates and then the recipe, using the quantity box.[/*]
[*][b]Queue[/b]: "Add to queue" and "Start queue" craft different recipes one after another, intermediates included. Remove an order with its x, or press Clear.[/*]
[*][b]Stop[/b]: nothing more is started and the queue is kept.[/*]
[*][b]Closing pauses the queue[/b]: close the workbench mid-run and the queue waits. Open the same workbench again and it carries on by itself. Crafts finished before the close are counted, so they are not made twice.[/*]
[*][b]Keep stocked[/b]: set how many of an item to keep (say 10 bandages) next to its recipe. Each time you open a workbench that makes it, the crafts that bring your home back up to that count are queued, and started if the queue was empty. Kept items show as chips with an x to stop keeping them.[/*]
[*][b]Game's own clicks[/b]: every craft goes through the workbench's own buttons, so the game's checks and timings apply.[/*]
[/list]

The mod adds no data of its own to your save (kept counts go in their own file in the BepInEx config folder), but the crafts it runs are real: they use materials from any storage in your home. You can remove it at any time; workbenches go back to their linked storages.

[size=5]Known issues[/size]
[list]
[*]Back up your save before trying it for the first time.[/*]
[*]Do not use it together with BetterCrafting. Remove BetterCrafting first.[/*]
[*]Intermediates must be made at the same workbench. The game only crafts at a workbench whose window is open, so the queue cannot use another one for you.[/*]
[*]The game cancels a craft when the workbench closes; that craft is made again when the queue carries on. Quitting the game empties the queue.[/*]
[*]A craft still going when you press Stop is not counted, so starting again can make that batch twice.[/*]
[*]Every storage in your home is used, including ones you meant to keep for something else.[/*]
[*]A kept item is only topped up at a workbench that makes it. Recipes that make several at once can end a little over the count.[/*]
[*]Kept counts follow the save file. A save the game starts fresh from an old one (rolling back, restarting from a snapshot) begins with none.[/*]
[/list]
If you run into anything strange, please tell me (see Feedback below).

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*]Survival Log 1.1.18317. A game update can break it until the mod is updated.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/survivallog/mods/12]BepInEx for Survival Log[/url]. Install it first.[/*]
[*]Not together with BetterCrafting.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/survivallog/mods/12]BepInEx[/url] [i](if no other mods were installed before, start the game once so BepInEx finishes setup, then quit)[/i].[/*]
[*]Put [font=Courier New]CraftingHub.dll[/font] in the BepInEx&#92;plugins folder inside the game folder. Full path example:
[code]C:&#92;Program Files (x86)&#92;Steam&#92;steamapps&#92;common&#92;Survival Log&#92;BepInEx&#92;plugins&#92;CraftingHub.dll[/code][/*]
[*]If you use BetterCrafting, delete its DLL from the same folder.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Uninstall[/size]
Delete [font=Courier New]CraftingHub.dll[/font] from the [font=Courier New]BepInEx&#92;plugins[/font] folder.

[size=5]Configuration[/size]
To turn off the whole-house storage, change how deep intermediates go, hide the marks, turn off keep stocked or its auto start, or have closing the workbench stop the queue instead of pausing it, edit [font=Courier New]BepInEx&#92;config&#92;com.dev.survivallog.craftinghub.cfg[/font] while the game is closed (see [url=https://github.com/AnExiledDev/SurvivalLog-CraftingHub/blob/main/CONFIG.md]CONFIG.md[/url]). Changes apply at the next game start.
